Which system holds your purchase register?

This agent reads invoices from your accounting system and the month's GSTR-2B from the GST portal. Pick where your books live.

The GST portal is connected separately in the next agent step — it uses your own login and is never stored.

How should Karya reach your Tally?

Tally runs on your machine, so Karya connects through a small secure bridge rather than holding your Tally password.

Grant exactly what the agent declared

These are the scopes from the agent's permission statement. Read access is on; you can't turn on anything it didn't ask for. Write access to your ledger was never requested.

You stay in control. Access lasts until you revoke it, and the agent can spend at most ₹25,000 a month under this grant. You can lower either in Billing at any time.

Point Karya at your Tally machine

Install the Karya Connector on the PC where Tally runs, then paste the pairing code it shows you. The connector holds the Tally link; Karya never sees a Tally password.

Open the Karya Connector on your Tally PC → it shows a 4-part code valid for 10 minutes.
So you can tell it apart if you add more branches later.
🔒
The link is stored in Karya's key vault, encrypted per company, and injected only into this agent's runs as a short-lived credential. It's never written to logs and never shared with the agent's developer.
